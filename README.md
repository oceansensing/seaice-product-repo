# seaice-product-repo

Satellite sea ice from the Copernicus Marine Service, as gridded data for the
oceansensing.org ocean map, each grid reaching as far toward its pole as its
satellites see: EUMETSAT OSI SAF's daily concentration, ice edge, ice type and
48-hour drift; DTU Space's Sentinel-1 SAR drift; DMI's 1 km concentrations and
the Arctic's sea and ice surface temperature; AWI's CryoSat-2 and SMOS
thickness; and FMI and SMHI's Baltic ice charts in the ice season.

Updated every three hours.
