# Decisions

Dated, irreversible-leaning decisions, one entry each, newest last. The
reasoning lives in `PLAN.md`.

**What counts as one-way in a data repository**: a decision that puts bytes in
readers' hands under a shape they will code against; a decision about which
repository owns a product, since moving one costs a migration in two places;
and a decision that forecloses an upstream.

## D1 — 2026-10-17 — Its own repository, R2 alone

Satellite sea ice gets a repository of its own (2026-10-17) and **publishes to Cloudflare R2 alone**, as every new product since the site pipeline's D13: Pages carries its status alone, and the website's map draws none of it. Its grids reach toward the poles as their satellites do, so a view of the polar oceans is never cut at the latitude a projection stops at.
